# Q3 Budget — Department Allocations

The Q3 budget totals Rs 1.2 crore across departments. Engineering gets
Rs 45 lakh, Sales Rs 30 lakh, Marketing Rs 25 lakh, and Operations
Rs 20 lakh. Any spend above a department's allocation needs finance
approval and a written justification.

Budget reviews happen on the first Monday of each month. Unspent budget
does not roll over to Q4 — use it or lose it. Vendor contracts above
Rs 2 lakh a year must go through the vendor review process before signing.
