# Holiday Calendar 2026

Company holidays in 2026: New Year's Day (1 Jan), Pongal (14–16 Jan),
Republic Day (26 Jan), Holi (3 Mar), Good Friday (3 Apr), Tamil New Year
(14 Apr), May Day (1 May), Independence Day (15 Aug), Ganesh Chaturthi
(14 Sep), Gandhi Jayanti (2 Oct), Diwali (8 Nov), and Christmas (25 Dec).

The office is closed on these days. If a holiday falls on a weekend, no
compensatory day off is given. Regional holidays for other states are
announced by HR one month in advance.
