# Expense Policy — What You Can Claim

Employees may claim work travel, client meals, and software subscriptions.
Submit claims in the expense tool within 30 days of spending, with a receipt
for anything over Rs 500.

Approval limits: team leads approve claims up to Rs 10,000. Claims above
Rs 10,000 need finance approval, and claims above Rs 50,000 need the CFO's
sign-off. International travel needs finance approval before booking.

Personal expenses, alcohol, and traffic fines are never reimbursable.
Reimbursements are paid within 7 working days of approval.
