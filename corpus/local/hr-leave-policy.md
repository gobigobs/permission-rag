# Leave Policy — Annual, Sick and Parental Leave

Every employee gets 21 days of annual leave, 12 days of sick leave, and
10 public holidays per year. Annual leave needs manager approval at least
7 days in advance; sick leave just needs a message to your manager on the
day itself.

Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 2 weeks for the
secondary caregiver, fully paid. Unused annual leave carries over up to
5 days into the next year; the rest lapses.

Leave balances are visible in the HR portal and update on the 1st of
every month.
